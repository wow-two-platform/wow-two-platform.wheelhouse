# v0.5 — Platform model

*Last updated: 2026-10-17*

**Status:** 🚧 In Progress · **Type:** Feature · **Started:** 2026-10-17 · **Completed:** —

### Iteration 1 — Workspaces and status

- [x] Ability to group products into workspaces
- [x] Ability to mark a product or a service active or inactive
- [x] Ability to show inactive products and services on demand

### Iteration 2 — Pins

- [x] Ability to pin workspaces, products and services
- [x] Ability to reorder pins

### Iteration 3 — Navigation and layout

- [x] Ability to navigate from a sidebar on desktop
- [x] Ability to navigate from a bottom bar on phones
- [x] Ability to see the whole portfolio on a home page
- [x] Ability to open a product on its own page
- [x] Ability to see services grouped by product and environment

### Iteration 4 — wow2 catalog

- [x] Ability to list every wow2 app in one catalog
- [x] Ability to import products from the catalog

### Iteration 5 — One manifest

- [ ] Ability to declare a product in one manifest file
- [ ] Ability to add a product without typing its images or release asset

### Iteration 6 — Settings and secrets

- [ ] Ability to see the settings and secrets each service declares
- [ ] Ability to edit settings per environment from allowed values
- [ ] Ability to see which required settings and secrets are missing

### Iteration 7 — Experience

- [ ] Ability to follow a deployment in a compact progress notification
- [ ] Ability to see rollout steps and page transitions animate
- [ ] Ability to swipe a table row on phones for its two main actions
- [ ] Ability to see an icon on each rollout step

### Iteration 8 — Two environments

- [ ] Ability to release from main to dev, then promote to prod

### Iteration 9 — Navigation by entity

- [ ] Ability to navigate from Home, Products, Deployments and Servers
- [ ] Ability to browse workspaces, then products, then services
- [ ] Ability to see a product's releases, secrets and activity on its page
- [ ] Ability to see which products each server hosts and affects

### Iteration 10 — Deployments

- [ ] Ability to see rollouts that are running now
- [ ] Ability to see deployments per day on an activity grid
- [ ] Ability to filter deployments by entity, status and date
- [ ] Ability to switch the deployments view between grid and list
- [ ] Ability to open one deployment on its own page
- [ ] Ability to promote releases that passed dev from one list

### Iteration 11 — Public hosting

- [ ] Ability to wire a products VPS with public ingress in one step
- [ ] Ability to route a product's sites through DNS records and TLS
- [ ] Ability to track domain and certificate expiry

### Iteration 12 — Backups

- [ ] Ability to back up databases and volumes off-provider on a schedule
- [ ] Ability to restore a backup and prove it works

### Iteration 13 — Logs

- [ ] Ability to collect every service's logs in one place
- [ ] Ability to search logs by product, service, level and time
- [ ] Ability to follow a service's logs live

### Iteration 14 — Monitoring and alerts

- [ ] Ability to probe every site's uptime from outside
- [ ] Ability to get alerts for downtime, certificates, disk and backups
- [ ] Ability to get notified of deploy outcomes

### Iteration 15 — Zero-disruption deploys

- [ ] Ability to drain requests and show maintenance before a rollout
- [ ] Ability to dry-run pending migrations before a rollout
- [ ] Ability for product frontends to refresh onto a new version
- [ ] Ability to deploy when a service is idle, or at a set time
